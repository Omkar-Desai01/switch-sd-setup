# Code of conduct

We want this to be a friendly, useful place for people at every experience level.

**Do:** be respectful and patient, give specific and kind feedback, assume good intent, and help newcomers.

**Don't:** harass, insult or discriminate against anyone; post other people's private information; or use the project
to promote piracy (requests for pirated games, keys or firmware are off topic and will be removed).

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block repeat
offenders. To report a problem, use the private reporting option described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or contact a
maintainer through their GitHub profile.
